# Image selection

Each active photograph must have its own source and placement, descriptive English/French alt text, and an explicit relevance reason in image-placements.json. Never use a shared fallback. Crops, colour changes and renames do not establish uniqueness.

Regional photographs must depict a locality covered by that guide. Where a verified relevant local photograph is unavailable, use neutral consultation or legal-preparation imagery without identifiable landmarks from another place. Do not depict another province’s courthouse as local context.

Practice-area images must support the actual subject: homes/keys/closing materials for property, housing/lease materials for tenancies, consultation for family, meetings/contracts for business, planning documents for estates, and court/research context for disputes. Remove unnecessary secondary images rather than add filler. Community navigation cards are text-led.

Before publishing image edits, inspect placement context and visuals; check source and file uniqueness plus perceptual/mirrored similarity against active site assets and accessible prior-site inventories. Record inaccessible inventory limitations privately. Keep provenance internal except required source licence attribution.
